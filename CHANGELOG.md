# Changelog

All notable changes to AirDeck are listed here. The format follows [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/), and versions follow [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/).

## [0.1.0] — 2026-11-23

First release.

### Added
- Send any YouTube tab to Deck A or B from the toolbar popup or with Alt+Shift+1 / Alt+Shift+2. An "On Deck" badge appears on the tab.
- Mixer tab with one shared audio engine: Bass / Vocals (approx.) / Treble switches with solo, a one-knob filter, a mix slider with three curves, channel and master volume, a limiter, auto level match, and ad muting.
- Tempo detection, Tap tempo, Sync, tempo slider (±8% / ±16%) and nudge.
- Eight one-tap mix styles: Smooth Fade, Bass Swap, Echo Out, Filter Fade, Riser Build, Quick Cut, Spinback and Brake, with Cancel.
- Loops (1/2/4/8 beats), four hot cues per deck remembered per song, and seven FX pads with an Amount knob.
- Four synthesized sample pads and four Grab pads.
- Live waveforms with beat ticks, level meters, key detection with Camelot codes, and a key-compatibility hint.
- Recording to WebM or WAV.
- Demo page with generated songs (also the Dev Harness), a welcome page, and help and settings dialogs.
- Unit tests (Vitest) and end-to-end tests (Playwright), including a real two-tab capture test.
